The Journey of Becoming

Day 1: The Messy Reality of Starting (and the 15K Goal)

📅 July 31, 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read 📍 Syangja, Nepal

The day didn’t start with the disciplined 4:00 AM wake-up I had envisioned. Instead, I opened my eyes at 7:00 AM. It felt like a failure right out of the gate, but I reminded myself that acknowledging the slip is the first step to correcting it.

My first intentional act of the day? Digital triage. I downloaded a few important short videos from Instagram for reference, and then immediately uninstalled the app. It was consuming too much of my mental bandwidth, and I needed to reclaim my focus.

A Grounding Moment

Earlier in the day, I had helped a friend find work at a local vegetable shop—the same kind of place I worked at just two months ago. Visiting him there was a deeply grounding experience. I even grabbed a mango and ate it without paying. It was a small, humble reminder of where I started, how far I’ve come, and the bonds that remain intact.

The 15K in 15 Days Quest

Back in my room, after finishing the downloads and permanently deleting the app, I ate my morning meal and faced the pressing question: How do I earn 15,000 NPR in the next 15 days?

I turned to ChatGPT and other AI tools, searching for a magic blueprint. When no exact, actionable plan materialized, a wave of hopelessness washed over me. I felt stuck.

In that fog of frustration, I remembered a postponed website project and the half-finished Meta Ads course I had been studying. A spark of an idea formed: What if I combine both and sell this as a service online?

But the friction returned. Prompting AI to execute this felt difficult and overwhelming. Defeated by the mental load, I ended up sleeping away a large part of the day.

The Nighttime Win

But the night brought clarity. I decided to start documenting this exact journey. I wrote down my raw, unfiltered thoughts, asked AI to help me structure and polish them, and then spent the evening editing.

By 9:00 PM, I had successfully uploaded a YouTube video. That part felt perfect. It was a tangible, undeniable win.

📌 Day 1 Takeaways

  • 1 Digital minimalism works: Uninstalling time-wasting apps creates immediate mental space and reduces friction.
  • 2 The "Learn Everything" trap: Trying to master relationships, finance, spirituality, and AI all at once leads to paralysis. Focus is required.
  • 3 Document, don't just create: Turning raw thoughts into a YouTube video by 9 PM proved that progress is still possible, even on a "wasted" day.

Looking Ahead

Reflecting on it all, I realize why today felt so heavy. Without a proper, focused plan, the day easily slips away. I still don’t have a perfect blueprint for tomorrow, but I’m choosing to show up anyway.

"I will earn that 15K. This is just Day 1."
